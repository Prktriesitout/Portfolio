Sleep Debt Analysis & ML Predictor

Built an end-to-end analysis and predictive modeling workspace exploring how demographics and evening habits (phone usage, caffeine, blue-light filters) affect sleep debt severity and next-day fatigue. Key findings: each extra hour of sleep lowers fatigue by about 1.6 points, and users with optimized evening habits averaged a 1.64 fatigue score versus 4.95 for those with none. Night owls (95.8%) and healthcare and shift workers (18.3% severe) are the most vulnerable groups.
